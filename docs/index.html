🌱 Beginner guide — read this and you are a professional

Let's work this out in a step-by-step way to be sure we have the right answer.

Step 1 — Macro: what country can pay?
Check GDP growth (World Bank), inflation (<2% good), SAMA rate (4.5% = restrictive). Peg 3.75 means Saudi mirrors America. High rates hurt builders, help banks — up to a point.
Step 2 — Sectors: who leads?
Banks 40% → credit first. Energy/materials → oil margins. Telecom/health/consumer → domestic demand (Vision 2030). REITs last (rate-sensitive). Never buy a stock before you name its sector.
Step 3 — Index math: index ≠ market.
TASI is cap-weighted: 2 giants can move 136 pts while 21 sectors fall. Like CIB in Egypt. Always check breadth + contributors, not just the headline.
Step 4 — P/E: how many years to get money back?
Price ÷ EPS. 100 ÷ 10 = 10 years. 100 ÷ 20 = 5 years. Low P/E (Bahri ~3–5×) is often a cyclical trough — check future margins, not just trailing.
Step 5 — P/B: book is not market.
Office 1m ÷ 20y depreciation → book 0, but market >1m. So P/B 2–3× is normal for good banks. P/B <1 (Cenomi 0.44) = flag distress or heavy assets — verify.
Step 6 — Fair value: buy the future.
Fair = future profits discounted. SNB fair 12,000 @16.1× vs 10,441 = ~15% upside if multiples normalise. Technicals tell when; fundamentals tell what + why.

You now know more than most interview candidates: order matters, multiples need context, concentration misleads.
