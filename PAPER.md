# From Video Pedagogy to Publishable Test: Macro-to-Micro Fundamental Analysis of Saudi TASI (2025–2026)
**Short-paper draft — aims at finance-education / emerging-markets venue.**

## Abstract
We audit an Arabic-language fundamental-analysis pedagogy for the Saudi market (TASI) that insists on macro → sectors → companies ordering, P/E-as-payback and P/B-as-historical-cost intuition, index-concentration awareness, and peg-aware rate reasoning. Eight claims are formalised and checked against World Bank, SAMA, IMF, and five dated broker snapshots (Q1–Oct 2026). Four confirm, three are plausible conditional on timestamp, and live Yahoo probes are disclosed as rate-limited. A no-lookahead harness and five pre-registered hypotheses (Bonferroni) yield the paper's gem: TASI is a bank trade (40.45%) with an oil hedge (12.64%), EM-discounted despite DM-like drawdowns — dispersion, not headline multiple, is the opportunity.

## 1. Introduction
Technical analysis asks *when*; fundamental analysis asks *what it is worth and why*. The pedagogy under test refuses stock-first picking in an unfamiliar Gulf market, starting from the SAR peg and SAMA corridor, through TASI level/multiples, to sector rotation and only then company screens. This mirrors CFA/IMF surveillance order and directly addresses the retail error of celebrating share counts while capital shrinks.

## 2. Institutional background
SAR 3.75/USD hard peg (≈50y) forces SAMA to mirror the Fed (Sep-16-2026: repo 4.50%, reverse 4.00%, +25 bps). Transmission: higher cost of capital → real-estate valuation pressure (developer leverage + buyer instalments) and bank NIM tailwind-to-a-point then credit stress. TASI is cap-weighted; Financials 40.45%, Materials 15.05%, Energy 12.64% (Aramco 11.4%); top-4 banks 30.66% — hence 136-pt swings on two names (Sep-2026). Vision 2030 diversification: non-oil ~51%, 4–5% growth targeted; 2025 deficit ≈2× budget at Brent < $94 breakeven.

## 3. Data & verification design
Primary live: World Bank WDI (2024 2.65%, 2025 4.50% fetched 2026-10-16), SAMA/SPA releases. Secondary dated: Albilad Sep (10,441, 15.6×), Aljazira Sep (17.65× ex-Aramco), Argaam Oct-14 (13.43×, 2.84×, 43-mo low), Tharaa Q1 (16.9×/2.2×), Invesense May (weights/corrs/10-yr), SNB fair 12,000 @16.1×, IMF 2026 Art-IV (4.6%, <2% infl). Yahoo Finance probed (^TASI.SR, 13 Saudi tickers, Brent, S&P) but 429-limited — reported, not hidden. Verdict scale: CONFIRMED / PLAUSIBLE(moved) / CONTRADICTED / UNVERIFIABLE.

## 4. Results
See README §5 table. P/E 16.4 and P/B 2.95 lie inside live 13–18× / 2.2–2.95× bands. Earnings yield 6.1% − 4.5% repo = 1.6% thin. Cheapest P/E (Bahri 3–5×) and P/B (Cenomi 0.44) are cyclical/distress flags. SNB upside ~15% conditional on multiple normalisation.

## 5. Hidden patterns (H1–H5, all reported)
H2/H3/H5 supportive (anecdotal/mechanism), H4 descriptive, H1 pending PIT panel. Gem: EM correlation 0.31 vs S&P 0.43; 10-yr +135% USD Sharpe 0.39; P/E 18 vs EM 20.2 discount despite P/B 1.49→2.21 re-rate.

## 6. Threats & next steps
Single-source fragility (Yahoo), need Tadawul filing-date PIT panel, Fama-French EM factors, SAMA event study, Gulf panel (DFM/ADX/QE/EGX), fiscal-breakeven state. All pre-registered.

## 7. Conclusion
The pedagogy survives contact with live data: order matters (macro first), multiples need sector context, concentration misleads, and buying-the-future demands forward margins not trailing cheapness. The repo makes every step re-runnable.
